### Selected work

**[latency-execution-engine](https://github.com/Nik-lesh/latency-execution-engine)**

Reinforcement learning for trade execution, built with a partner. Double DQN and QR-DQN agents minimising slippage on large crypto orders across 7.5M bars and 98M tick-level trades. +10.2 bps against TWAP on BTCUSDT out of sample, and an honest write-up of where it loses: SOLUSDT is negative. 176 tests, CI on every push.

**[language-model-evolution](https://github.com/Nik-lesh/language-model-evolution)**

RNN, LSTM and Transformer written from scratch and benchmarked on financial text. The finding that mattered: data composition beat architecture. Rebalancing an 83% classical-economics corpus toward modern sources improved loss 24%, more than any hyperparameter change.

**[DINOv2](https://github.com/Nik-lesh/DINOv2)**

Fine-tuning Meta's DINOv2 ViT with two-phase progressive training, differential learning rates, MixUp, CutMix and EMA. k-NN and linear probing, t-SNE, and out-of-distribution robustness testing at ~50 FPS inference.
